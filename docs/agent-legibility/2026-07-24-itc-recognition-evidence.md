# Claims / Evidence — "UN ITC recognized" (GoviHub presentation)

**Date recorded:** 2026-07-24 · **Surface:** `govihublk.com/presentation/` (og:description)

## The claim

The presentation's social-share metadata (`og:description`) states, in Sinhala:
*"UN ITC පිළිගත් ශ්‍රී ලාංකික AI ගොවිතැන් වේදිකාව"* — "UN ITC recognized Sri Lankan AI
farming platform."

## The evidence — SUBSTANTIATED

An invitation received from the **International Trade Centre (ITC)**, from an `@intracen.org`
address, states that **"GoviHub: AI-Driven Smart Farming Marketplace for Sri Lanka has been selected
as one of the outstanding cases from the Young Entrepreneurs' Digital Innovation Case Collection."**
On that basis GoviHub was invited to ITC's **Silk Road E-commerce Best Practices Training Programme**
in China (14–27 September 2026), fully funded, run under the ITC–AIBO partnership.

**Why "UN ITC" is accurate:** ITC is the **joint agency of the United Nations and the World Trade
Organization** (Geneva; `intracen.org`). Describing it as a UN agency is correct.

## Assessment

- **"Recognized" is defensible.** Selection as an outstanding case in an ITC-curated collection is a
  genuine recognition by a UN agency.
- **Precision would make it stronger, not weaker.** "UN recognized" read alone could be misheard as
  a broad UN endorsement/certification of the product. The specific, verifiable version —
  *selected by ITC as an outstanding case in its Young Entrepreneurs' Digital Innovation Case
  Collection* — is both more credible and unassailable. Recommended, pending founder decision on
  wording. This is a positioning choice, not a correctness fix; the current wording is not false.
- **Scope limit:** only the HTML `og:description` was auditable/editable. The 19 slide images are
  baked `.webp` and their internal wording could not be inspected here.

## Deliberately excluded from this record

The source invitation contains personal data — the named recipient, a **single-use personal
registration link** (`surveys.intracen.org/response/<token>`), and medical/visa/travel requirements.
None of it is recorded here and **none of it may appear on any public page or be shared onward.** The
personal registration link must not be clicked or distributed — it is tied to one individual's
invitation.

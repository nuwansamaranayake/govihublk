# Truth Layer — GoviHub Terms of Use v1.0 + match liability disclaimer

**Date:** 2026-07-19 · **Branch:** `spices` · **Subject type:** company/product (pilot marketplace)
**Scope:** claims made by the ToS legal text and the match-card disclaimer, audited against what the
platform actually does. Companion to [`TOS_AUDIT.md`](../../TOS_AUDIT.md) (system audit).

Evidence quality: **strong** — every verdict below is grounded in repo source, not description.

---

## 1. Diagnosis

The ToS is unusually *honest* for a marketplace: sections 3, 4, and 9 correctly describe GoviHub as a
non-party introducer, and the code backs that up — there is **no payment processing anywhere**, no
escrow, no commission, no fee-taking, no custody of produce. That is the strongest part of the
document and it is fully defensible.

The weak point is section 6, and it fails in an unexpected direction: it claims **more AI than
exists**. Matching — the single feature this entire liability disclaimer is built around — is 100%
deterministic weighted arithmetic. Nothing about it is AI.

Two documents will also now describe the same platform: the existing `govihub-umbrella/public/terms.html`
and the new in-app `/terms`. Conflicting legal texts are worse than one imperfect one.

## 2. Truth Layer — canonical facts

| Capability | What it actually is | AI-backed? |
|---|---|---|
| Crop diagnosis | Gemini 2.0 Flash vision via OpenRouter (`diagnosis/service.py:135,161`) | **Yes — genuine LLM** |
| Advisory answers | Embeddings → pgvector RAG → OpenRouter LLM (`advisory/service.py:115,147`) | **Yes — genuine LLM + RAG** |
| Weather | Open-Meteo third-party API; alerts rule-based (`weather/service.py:15`) | **No — external API** |
| Match suggestions | Weighted score: distance .35 / quantity .25 / date overlap .25 / freshness .15, pure arithmetic + PostGIS (`matching/engine.py:22-26,338`) | **No — deterministic** |

**Constraints that are true and worth stating plainly:**
- No payment processor, escrow, wallet, commission, or fee logic exists. Verified: zero hits for
  stripe/payhere/paypal/escrow/checkout/commission/payout across api, web, and admin.
- `agreed_price_per_kg` is **recorded metadata only** — no money moves through GoviHub.
- No warehousing, courier, or shipment code. `delivery_available` / `delivery_radius_km` are
  **farmer-declared attributes**, not a GoviHub service.
- Ads carry **no price, budget, bid, or fee column** (`010_add_advertisements.py:19-37`); they are
  admin-created only and never boost listings or matches. Ordering is `display_order, random()`.

**Boundaries (the "not for" that protects us):** GoviHub does not verify identity, inspect goods,
guarantee payment, or mediate disputes — and sections 3, 5, and 9 say so correctly.

## 4. AI-Washing Risk Register

**R1 — §6 implies AI-generated matching that does not exist. (HIGH — the notable finding)**
Matching is a fixed four-term weighted score. The sentence groups four features and attributes them
to "software, including artificial intelligence." Read collectively it is technically survivable —
two of the four *are* LLM-backed. Read by a farmer, it says *the matches are AI*.
This is textbook AI-washing: the product sounds more advanced than the evidence supports.
It is also the *opposite* of the usual legal-text failure — an over-claim inside a disclaimer.
**Note:** the spec declares the English text FINAL, so this is escalated, not edited. A minimal
truthful repair would be "generated by software, including artificial intelligence for crop
diagnosis and advisory answers" — bounded, still fully protective, no longer over-claiming.

**R2 — ToS says "guidance only"; the diagnosis UI does not. (MEDIUM)**
`farmer/diagnosis/page.tsx:258-267` renders `detectedDisease`, "Recommended Treatments", and a bold
"High Confidence" percentage bar with **no standing disclaimer**. `consultExpert` renders only
conditionally (`:243`). Advisory UI has zero disclaimer copy. A ToS clause that the interface
contradicts on screen is weak evidence of informed acceptance. Out of scope here; logged as backlog.

**R3 — Two conflicting Terms documents will exist. (MEDIUM)**
`govihub-umbrella/public/terms.html` already publishes terms for the same platform. Shipping a
second, different ToS at `/terms` creates ambiguity about which governs — precisely the problem
section 12's language-precedence clause exists to avoid. Needs reconciliation.

**R4 — §14 support mailbox: accepted at SMTP, destination unproven. (LOW-MEDIUM — partially resolved)**
`support@govihublk.com` is the ToS's only contact channel, but `.env.example:42` sends from
`reports@govihublk.com` and `docs/audits/RESEND_VERIFICATION.md:68` calls support@ aspirational.

> **VERIFIED 2026-07-19** (DNS + live SMTP probe, no message sent — `RCPT TO` then `QUIT`, no `DATA`):
> - `MX govihublk.com` → `inbound-smtp.us-east-1.amazonaws.com` (**AWS SES inbound**)
> - `MAIL FROM` → `250 Ok`; `RCPT TO: support@govihublk.com` → **`250 Ok`**
> - **Control:** `RCPT TO: definitely-not-real-xyz@govihublk.com` → **also `250 Ok`**
>
> **Interpretation — do not over-read this.** The control address accepting proves the probe cannot
> distinguish a real mailbox from a domain-wide catch-all. What is now established: mail to support@
> is **accepted and will not bounce**. What is still **unproven**: that any SES receipt rule routes it
> to a destination a human reads. Confirming that needs the AWS console (SES → receipt rule set →
> S3/Lambda/forward target); no AWS credentials are available in this session.
>
> Net: better than the prior "may silently drop mail" — mail is not rejected at the edge. But §14
> should not be called verified. **Open item for Nuwan: confirm the SES receipt rule destination.**
>
> Also noted: **no SPF TXT record on the apex** (`dig TXT govihublk.com` → empty), and
> `_dmarc` is `p=none`. Outbound deliverability concern, unrelated to the ToS but worth logging.

**R5 — Pre-existing false claim, unchanged by decision. (MEDIUM)**
`auth.termsAgreement` asserts users agree to a **Privacy Policy that has no in-app route**
(`/privacy` → 404). Manager decision 2026-07-19: flag only, do not fix this run.

**R6 — `privacy.html` contradicts the code. (MEDIUM — outside this run)**
It states deleted accounts are *"hard-deleted from production"*, but `users/models.py:47` `deleted_at`
is a **soft delete**. It also broadly denies advertising use of data, while ads *are* first-party
targeted by role and district with per-user impression logging (`010:46-52`). Both are fixable by
precision, not by policy change.

## 5. Human Memory Layer

The line worth protecting is already written and it is good:

> **"GoviHub only connects you. The sale, payment, and delivery are between you and the other party."**

Plain, true, no hedging, survives translation, and a farmer remembers it. It does the legal work of
sections 3, 4, and 9 in one sentence. Keep it verbatim; do not let it get "improved."

The credible technical wedge is **not** "AI marketplace" — it is: *diagnosis and advisory are
genuine LLM features; matching is transparent, explainable arithmetic you could check by hand.*
Explainable matching is a **stronger** story with farmers than a black box. R1 trades a real
differentiator for a generic one.

## 8. Decisions (Nuwan, 2026-07-19)

**R1 — bound the AI claim to what is true.** §6 ships as:

> "Crop diagnosis, advisory answers, weather information, and match suggestions are generated by
> software, including artificial intelligence for crop diagnosis and advisory answers."

This is the **only** authorized deviation from the "FINAL" English text in the spec, and it is a
narrowing, not a rewrite — the disclaimer's protective scope is unchanged, it simply stops implying
AI-generated matching. All three translations must carry the same bounded meaning.

**R3 — the new in-app `/terms` is canonical.** After deploy, `govihub-umbrella/public/terms.html`
is pointed at it rather than left as a competing text. This touches the **umbrella stack**
(`docker-compose.umbrella.yml` on `govihub-mumbai`), which is a separate deploy from spices — it is
sequenced *after* spices verification, not bundled into it.

**R4 — verify before deploy.** Done, partially: mail is accepted, destination unproven. §14 ships
as written. Open item: confirm the SES receipt rule destination.

**Bottom line:** ship the ToS. Its liability architecture is sound and evidence-backed — no payment
handling, no custody, no paid ranking, and the match disclaimer copy is accurate. R1 is resolved by
a one-clause narrowing; R3 by making the in-app document canonical; R4 is de-risked but not closed.
Remaining known defects (R2, R5, R6) are logged, none block this deploy.

## Context: the state of Tamil in this app

`ta.json` currently holds **259 strings, of which 198 are still untranslated English placeholders marked `[TA] ...` — only about 24% of the app is genuinely translated into Tamil.**

The practical consequence: **this Terms of Use will be the most complete Tamil text in the product.** A Tamil-speaking user will read a fully translated legal document sitting inside an app shell that is still largely English. That is a jarring experience and worth planning for.

Backfilling those placeholders was explicitly **out of scope** for this task and none of them were touched. The three new `auth` keys and the new `tos` and `matches` entries are real Tamil, not placeholders.

Note also that `ta.json` had **no `matches` namespace at all** before this change. It was created containing only the three disclaimer keys — the other ~31 `matches` keys that exist in English and Sinhala are still absent from Tamil.
